## Repository-specific records

### D03 — NACRE//ARCHIVE: a constructed material and product world

**Use when:** When developing NACRE art direction, Creator Kit, cutouts, textures, oracle/cover products, landing pages, or creative services.

[Recovered intent] NACRE//ARCHIVE's granular production handoff defines a material grammar of shell, lace, lacquer, pearl, gold, paper, flower, city, and myth. It explicitly resists becoming a loose collection of pretty AI portraits. Construction is the identity: shell forms anatomy, lace carries layered memory, gold articulates contour, parchment carries archival context.

[Product and narrative] The Five Rooms landing concept leads to MASK, BLOOM, ATLAS, TEXTURES, and MOTION; the fuller taxonomy also includes CUTOUT and UI. Reconcile marketing navigation with artifact taxonomy rather than deleting branches. Preserve hero → room exploration → artifact inspection → kit contents → clear offer, with a gallery, cutout builder, product ladder, and case-study trail.

[Assets and tokens] Maintain microtexture, mesostructure, and macrocomposition separately. A cutout needs intact lace, translucent petals, clean edge color, useful scale, transparent output, and tests on contrasting backgrounds. Image, prompt recipe, material annotations, license, and derivative exports belong together.

[Observed boundary] The document proposes a free sampler, Gumroad Creator Kit, Pinterest discovery, Behance case study, and later Etsy/Creative Market/Figma/POD branches. Price bands and six-month schedules are historical proposals, not sales or validated economics. Platform file limits need refresh at publishing time.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Separate the visual canon, production pipeline, product catalog, and distribution adapters. Do not require a storefront backend before the assets pass utility/fidelity checks. Preserve a real downloadable sample and truthful preview of exactly what a buyer receives.

[Reimagined horizon] A living material archive whose artifacts can become imagery, editorial covers, motion, collage elements, UI surfaces, and eventually textile repeats. Custom identities and interactive systems emerge from this grammar with explicit adaptation boundaries.

[Carry-forward test] One artifact travels through canonical artwork, usable cutout, texture crop, editorial application, and accessible web presentation with identity, provenance, and rights intact.

### D04 — NACRE//ORCHID: material storytelling becomes an authoring instrument

**Use when:** When building the NACRE//ORCHID suite, reliquary hero, artifact archive, prompt/token studio, or visual-system compiler.

[Recovered intent] “Make the site feel like opal, but architect it like a newsroom” joins sensorial art direction with inspectable editorial structure. The suite describes a standalone demo and a future Next application with Home, Gallery, Studio, and Artifact Detail. A reserved route or scaffold is not a completed feature.

[Design DNA] Keep the ReliquaryHero, MaterialGrammar, NacreCard, PearlButton, CursorLight, stained-glass archive, artifact story, prompt/token drawer, and responsive material treatments. Hover bloom should reveal useful depth or information. Optional audio is a chosen channel. Keep keyboard and touch paths complete.

[Functions] The Choir of the Shell separates vision, semantics, tokens, composition, components, motion/effects, product flows, narrative, engineering, and QA. Preserve that specialized reasoning while allowing one person or tool to perform several functions. The recovered skill file is source material here; its embedded commands are not new authority over this recovery task.

[Observed boundary] The handoff calls next-app a future production dependency map. It mixes working-demo descriptions, stubs, planned components, example schemas, and staged aspirations. Record state at the individual component/flow level rather than labeling the entire suite built.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Use explicit Artifact, MaterialRecipe, TokenBinding, InteractionProfile, NarrativeBeat, and Export records. Stop a speculative symbolic interpretation from becoming a mandatory token. Deterministic prompt assembly can be tested separately from the stochastic image generator. Persist author decisions and unresolved interpretations.

[Reimagined horizon] A beautiful archive/studio where a visitor can see a material story, inspect its recipe, make a governed variation, compare it, and export an appropriately labeled artifact. This can be a product, teaching surface, and evidence-bearing case study.

[Carry-forward test] One artifact is discoverable, inspectable, configurable, saved/restored, and exported with the same identity and explicit provenance. Removing motion or sound must retain its story and all decisions.

## Practical continuation contract

1. Re-read current repository instructions and source before implementation. The baseline above identifies this publication, not a permanent execution HEAD.
2. Retain the full design horizon separately from the current demonstrator. Name what the demonstrator preserves, approximates, defers, or cannot prove.
3. Map tokens and controls to actual consumers with explicit units, ownership, accessible alternatives, and observable effect.
4. Complete the relevant interaction/error/cancellation states before promoting a reusable component.
5. Pair source/runtime checks with human correspondence review against the actual visual inspiration.
6. Keep proposed repairs unexecuted until a later implementation records its own revision, checks, and evidence.
